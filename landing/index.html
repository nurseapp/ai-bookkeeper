FAQ

Common questions

How does it work?
Send a photo of your receipt to our WhatsApp number. Our AI reads the receipt, extracts all details, categorizes the expense, and saves it to your personal Google Sheet. You get a confirmation message instantly.
Do I need to install an app?
No. It works entirely through WhatsApp, which you already have. Just save our number and start sending receipts.
What types of receipts does it support?
Any printed or digital receipt. Restaurant bills, grocery receipts, gas stations, online order confirmations — if you can photograph it, we can read it. Works with multiple currencies too.
Where is my data stored?
All your expense data is stored in a Google Sheet that you have full access to. You can view, edit, export, or download it anytime. We never sell your data.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes, cancel anytime from your Stripe billing portal. No contracts, no cancellation fees.
What commands can I use?
Text "help" for all commands. Includes: summary, budget, total, last 5 (recent transactions), delete last, and categories. All via simple WhatsApp messages.
